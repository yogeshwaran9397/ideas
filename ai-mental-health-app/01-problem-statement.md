# Problem Statement

## AI-Based Mental Health Monitoring Application

### Problem Overview

Modern users spend significant time on laptops and mobile devices, often resulting in stress, anxiety, and reduced productivity. Current applications rarely provide real-time monitoring or actionable interventions to help users manage their mental state.

### Problem Statement

In today's digital-first world, users face increasing levels of stress and anxiety due to prolonged device usage, multitasking, and demanding work schedules. The lack of real-time mental health monitoring and intervention tools leaves users unaware of their deteriorating mental state until it significantly impacts their productivity and well-being.

**Key Issues Identified:**
- Users are unaware of their escalating stress levels during device usage
- No real-time feedback mechanism for mental state assessment
- Lack of timely, personalized interventions for stress management
- Limited tools that work seamlessly across laptops and mobile devices
- Privacy concerns with existing mental health applications

### Target Goals

Develop a standalone AI application that addresses these issues by:

1. **Real-time Monitoring**: Continuously monitors user behavior and interactions on laptops/mobile devices
2. **Mental State Assessment**: Uses AI to assess mental state and anxiety levels based on behavioral patterns
3. **Timely Interventions**: Provides personalized, context-aware interventions to improve well-being and engagement
4. **Privacy-First Approach**: Ensures user data privacy with local processing capabilities
5. **Cross-Platform Compatibility**: Works on standard laptops and mobile devices without additional hardware

### Key Objectives

- **Real-time mental state monitoring** through behavioral analysis
- **Detection of high stress or anxiety patterns** using AI/ML algorithms
- **Interactive, personalized interventions** to reset the user's mental state
- **Minimal hardware dependency** (works on standard laptops and mobile devices)
- **Privacy-conscious handling** of user data with local processing

### Success Metrics

- Accurate detection of stress/anxiety levels (>85% accuracy)
- Timely intervention delivery (within 30 seconds of detection)
- User engagement with suggested interventions (>60% compliance rate)
- Improvement in user-reported well-being scores
- Minimal system resource usage (<5% CPU, <100MB RAM)

### Scope Limitations

**Out of Scope:**
- Clinical diagnosis or medical treatment recommendations
- Complex hardware sensors (EEG, heart rate monitors, etc.)
- Multi-user network integration
- Integration with existing medical systems
- Real-time video/audio analysis (beyond optional emotion detection)

### Target Users

- **Primary**: Office workers, students, remote workers
- **Secondary**: Freelancers, content creators, researchers
- **Demographics**: Adults aged 18-55 who spend 4+ hours daily on digital devices
